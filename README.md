# TrailCast

TrailCast wants to be your hike companion, to let you help figure out when to start your hike, which hike to do, or just if you should take that extra jacket or not. It takes a given hike and calculates the weather forecast along your hike, based on where you would be at a certain time.

## Features

- **Hike Management:** Track and organize your hiking trips. (TODO)
- **Weather Forecasting:** Get up-to-date weather information for your planned routes. (WIP)
- **Map Integration:** Visualize your hikes and explore new trails on interactive maps. (WIP)
- **Data Upload:** Easily upload and manage your hike data. (TODO)
- **Responsive Design:** Enjoy a consistent experience across various devices with Tailwind CSS and Vuetify.
